Genshinkan Aikido, NYC is a member of the international organization, Aikido World Alliance, which is headed by Andrew Sato Shihan, and is directly affiliated with Aikikai Foundation, Aikido World Headquarters in Tokyo, Japan.

About Andrew Sato Shihan

Andrew Sato Shihan, 7th Dan

Andrew Sato Shihan, 7th dan, is the founder and chief instructor of the AWA. Sato Shihan and several dedicated dojo and students started the organization in 2005. Under his leadership, the AWA has grown to more than 50 dojo worldwide, and is one of only a few US-based aikido associations directly affiliated with the Aikikai Foundation, Aikido World Headquarters, in Tokyo, Japan.

As head instructor of the AWA, Sato Shihan travels the world regularly giving seminars. He also regularly teaches at Kiku Matsu Dojo, Chicago, which he started in 2005. It is now under the direction of Laura DeGraff Sensei, 6th dan.

AWA Member Dojo

Sato Shihan began his study of aikido with the late Fumio Toyoda Shihan in Chicago in 1977.

Following Toyoda Shihan's passing in 2001, Sato Shihan founded the Aikido World Alliance in 2005, continuing the transmission of traditional Aikido while maintaining direct affiliation with the Aikikai Foundation in Tokyo, Japan.

About the Organization

International Reach

The Aikido World Alliance connects dojos across multiple continents, maintaining the highest standards of traditional Aikido instruction through regular seminars and consistent technical development.

Direct Connection to Aikikai

Through Sato Shihan's regular training at Hombu Dojo in Tokyo and direct instruction from Doshu Moriteru Ueshiba, AWA maintains a strong connection to the source of Aikido.

Traditional Training

The AWA maintains traditional Aikido training methods with regular seminars and consistent technical development across all member dojos.
