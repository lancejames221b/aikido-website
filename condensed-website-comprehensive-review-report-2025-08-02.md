## Condensation Strategy Applied

### 🔄 CONDENSED SECTIONS

#### 1. Hero Section - Before vs After
**BEFORE**: Extensive marketing copy with multiple value propositions
**AFTER**: 
```
Traditional Aikido in the East Village
Embrace Shoshin 初心
The Beginner's Mind

"In the beginner's mind there are many possibilities,
but in the expert's mind there are few."
— Shunryu Suzuki Roshi
```

#### 2. Training Information - Before vs After
**BEFORE**: Lengthy descriptions of benefits and outcomes
**AFTER**: Direct, factual presentation:
- Manhattan Dojo location and address
- Class schedule (exact times)
- New Student Rate: $115/month (includes unlimited classes)
- What You'll Practice (bulleted list)
- "All levels welcome. No experience necessary."

#### 3. Philosophy Section - Before vs After
**BEFORE**: Extensive philosophical explanations
**AFTER**: Three focused cards:
- Shoshin (初心) - Beginner's Mind
- Rei (礼) - Honor & Respect  
- Heiwa (平和) - Peace & Harmony
Each with "Learn More →" linking to detailed philosophy pages

#### 4. Testimonials - Before vs After
**BEFORE**: 6+ detailed testimonials with demographic targeting
**AFTER**: 2 curated, authentic quotes:
- "After 5+ years at Genshinkan, I can say this is excellent Aikido instruction. Gary Sensei maintains the lineage while creating a welcoming environment."
- "The dojo has people from all walks of life. Everyone is friendly, welcoming, and unpretentious."

#### 5. Contact Form - Before vs After
**BEFORE**: Extensive lead qualification and AI processing
**AFTER**: Simple, direct form:
- Name, Email, Phone
- Martial Arts Experience (dropdown)
- Questions/Comments
- Single CTA: "Schedule Free Training Session"

## Philosophy Alignment

This condensation follows the **"less is more" budo approach** inspired by genaikido.com's minimal communication style:

1. **Directness over Marketing**: Facts rather than sales language
2. **Substance over Style**: Real content over promotional messaging  
3. **Respect for Student Journey**: Individual discovery rather than demographic targeting
4. **Traditional Integrity**: Maintaining lineage and authentic terminology
